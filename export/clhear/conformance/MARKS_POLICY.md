# Conformance marks and usage policy

Licence: CC BY 4.0. Version 1.0. This policy applies the CLHEAR trademark policy (`../governance/TRADEMARK_POLICY.md`) to the four conformance marks. Where the two differ, the trademark policy governs.

## The marks

| Mark | Word form | Granted by |
|---|---|---|
| CLHEAR CL1 Mapped | "CLHEAR CL1 Mapped" | program verifier, on a verified Annex E self-assessment |
| CLHEAR CL2 Traceable | "CLHEAR CL2 Traceable" | program verifier, on a verified Annex E self-assessment |
| CLHEAR CL3 Assessed | "CLHEAR CL3 Assessed" | program, on an accredited assessor's ISAE 3000 report |
| CLHEAR CL4 Automated | "CLHEAR CL4 Automated" | program, on an accredited assessor's ISAE 3000 report |

Logo files for the marks are issued to the mark holder with the grant and may not be altered.

## What a mark permits

A holder may state, in the word form above and always with the scope statement, that the named program conforms at that level:

> CLHEAR CL2 Traceable — AML/CTF program, Example Bank plc UK entities, release clhear-v2026.09.01, valid to 2027-09-12. Register: https://clhear.org/conformance/register#CFM-000123

The statement may appear in regulatory correspondence, tender responses, annual reports, websites and product documentation of the holder. The register entry id must be included or linked wherever the mark appears so a reader can verify it.

## What a mark does not permit

- Using a mark for a scope, entity, program or release other than the one on the register.
- Using a mark after its `valid_to` date or after withdrawal.
- Implying that CLHEAR, Reg42 or the steering group endorses the holder, its products or its compliance with any law.
- Using "CLHEAR conformant", "CLHEAR certified", "CL1"–"CL4" or similar for anything not on the register, including while a self-assessment is pending.
- A vendor stating that its *product* "is CL4": marks attach to an organisation's program. A vendor may say its product was used within a program that holds a mark, with the register id.
- Sub-licensing the mark, or using it in a way that suggests a different level than granted.

## Scope statements

Every use carries: level and name; program; organisation and entities; CLHEAR release; validity end date; register id. Abbreviated use (a badge) must link to the register entry.

## Validity, renewal, withdrawal

- Validity: 12 months from grant.
- Renewal: a new self-assessment (CL1/CL2) or assessment (CL3/CL4) before expiry; a renewal may name a newer release.
- Withdrawal: on material change of scope or profile, lapse of the annual review, a failed reconciliation window, a report the program finds materially wrong, misuse of the mark, or at the holder's request. The program records the reason; the entry stays on the register as withdrawn. The holder removes the mark from new material within 30 days and from existing digital material within 90 days.
- Appeal: to the steering group under `../governance/CHARTER.md`, within 30 days of a decline or withdrawal.

## The register

https://clhear.org/conformance/register (`GET /conformance/register`) is the only authoritative list of granted marks. It shows, for every mark ever granted: id, level, organisation, program, scope, release, blueprint, granted-by (program verifier, or assessor register id for CL3/CL4), grant date, validity and status. It never shows the organisation's evidence.

## Fees

CL1 and CL2 verification is free. CL3 and CL4 assessment fees are set by the assessor; the program charges no fee for recording a mark. Reg42 receives no share of assessor fees.

## Enforcement

Misuse is first addressed by a request to correct within 14 days. Continued misuse is enforced under the trademark policy; the steering group is informed of every enforcement action and it is minuted.
