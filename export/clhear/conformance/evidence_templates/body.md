# Evidence template — Body (committee, board)

Shows that a required body exists and functions.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Reference | terms of reference id |
| Obligations | `OBL-` ids or the `ITM-` id |
| Mandate | as in the terms of reference |
| Membership and quorum | compare with characteristics `membership`, `quorum` |
| Cadence | compare with `meeting_cadence` |
| Reporting line | to which body it reports |

Operating evidence over a period: minutes for each meeting in the period showing quorum and the matters the mandate requires.
