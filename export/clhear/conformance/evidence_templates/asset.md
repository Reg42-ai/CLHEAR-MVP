# Evidence template — Asset (register, funds, records)

Shows that a required asset is held and maintained.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Reference | register id, account or ledger reference |
| Obligations | `OBL-` ids or the `ITM-` id |
| Content or amount | what the register holds / the amount held — compare with the blueprint's threshold |
| Maintenance | who updates it and how often |
| Retention | compare with `retention` |

Operating evidence over a period: reconciliations or attestations at the stated cadence; retention evidence.
