# Evidence template — Role

Shows that a required role is appointed and meets the blueprint's characteristics.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Reference | appointment letter id, HR record id, regulator approval reference |
| Obligations | `OBL-` ids or the `ITM-` id |
| Holder | role title (a name only if the regulator requires it) |
| Seniority | reporting line and grade — compare with `seniority`, `reporting_line` |
| Independence | from the business lines overseen — compare with `independence` |
| Competence | qualifications, training record — compare with `competence` |
| Appointment date and any regulator notification | |

Operating evidence over a period: continuity of appointment; training records; escalations made.
