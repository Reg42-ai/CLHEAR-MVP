# CLHEAR Code of Conduct

Version 1.0 · licence CC BY 4.0

## Our pledge

We, as contributors, reviewers, maintainers and steering-group members, pledge to make participation in CLHEAR a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community — and, because this is a standard people rely on to comply with the law, to argue from evidence.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Citing the source. Disagreement about an obligation is settled by the clause, the effective date and the why-trail, not by seniority.
- Reviewing the contribution, not the contributor. A reject carries a reason the contributor can act on.
- Declaring conflicts of interest before reviewing or voting (see `CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md`).
- Being respectful of differing opinions, jurisdictions and professional traditions.
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission.
- Presenting a commercial interest as a technical argument, or using a role in the community to obtain a commercial preference.
- Submitting text you do not have the right to republish, or knowingly submitting a false correction.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject forum posts, RFCs, contributions and reviews that do not align with this Code, and will communicate reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in all community spaces — the GitHub repository and Discussions, the Discourse forum, the monthly open call, the newsletter, working-group meetings — and when an individual is officially representing the community in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at conduct@clhear.org. Reports involving a maintainer go to the steering-group chair at chair@clhear.org. All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Reporters' privacy and security are respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

| Level | Community impact | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour | Private written warning with clarity about the violation and why it was inappropriate; a public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | A violation through a single incident or series of actions | Warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with the people involved for a specified period, including community spaces and external channels |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour | Temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period; roles suspended for the period |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals; knowingly submitting false corrections | Permanent ban from any public interaction within the community; roles revoked (the record of past contributions stays, per the standard's no-deletion rule) |
