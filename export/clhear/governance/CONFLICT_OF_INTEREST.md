# Conflict-of-interest rules

Version 1.0 · licence CC BY 4.0

The standard is vendor-neutral by rule. These rules make that operational for reviewers, maintainers and steering-group members.

## 1. What counts as a conflict

A person has a conflict on a matter when the outcome could reasonably affect:

- their employer, client or a company in which they (or a close family member) hold a material interest;
- a product or service they sell, including compliance software, consulting or assurance services that compete with or depend on the matter;
- a regulatory proceeding, enforcement action or litigation they or their employer is party to;
- their own contribution (an author never reviews their own work — this is enforced by the flow).

Reg42 staff have a standing conflict on matters that concern Reg42's commercial layers (instance mode, benchmarks, Reg42 OS) and on the conformance assessment of Reg42's own products.

## 2. Declaration

- Reviewers and maintainers file a **standing declaration** on their profile: employer, material interests, assurance or consulting relationships with regulated firms in the clusters they work in. It is public.
- Before reviewing a contribution or voting on a matter, the person checks the declaration against the matter and **declares a specific conflict** in the review note or the vote record if one exists.
- Declarations are updated within 30 days of a change.

## 3. Consequences of a conflict

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Reviewing a contribution | A conflicted reviewer does not review it. If discovered later, the review is voided and the contribution needs a replacement accept. |
| Maintainer escalation decision | The conflicted maintainer hands the decision to another maintainer of the cluster or, failing that, to the steering-group chair. |
| Steering-group vote | The conflicted member declares and abstains; abstention is recorded. Quorum is computed without them. |
| Golden-set curation | A conflicted person may propose cases but not accept them into the gate. |
| Conformance assessment | An assessor never assesses a firm they have advised in the previous 24 months. |

## 4. Reg42 specifically

- Reg42's steering seats vote on charter, licences and roadmap like any other seat, and abstain on conformance decisions concerning Reg42 products.
- Reg42 may not obtain a preference in the standard, the schema or the evals by virtue of operating the fleets. Any change to a golden set proposed by Reg42 staff needs two non-Reg42 reviewers.
- Commercial data (instance mode, member benchmarks) never enters the open store; the release scan for organization identifiers is public evidence of this.

## 5. Enforcement

Undeclared conflicts are a Code of Conduct matter (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, level 2 or above). Decisions taken under an undeclared conflict are re-taken.
