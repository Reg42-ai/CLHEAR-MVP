Version 1.0 · licence CC BY 4.0

The open licences on the standard's text, data and code (CC BY 4.0, ODC-By 1.0, Apache-2.0) do **not** grant rights in the Marks.

## You may, without asking

- Refer to the standard by name: "built on CLHEAR", "imports CLHEAR blueprints", "CLHEAR obligation OBL-000123".
- State facts: "our program was composed with CLHEAR release 2026.10.02".
- Use the name in scholarship, journalism, commentary and comparative statements.
- Use the name in the title of a compatible SDK or tool as "*X* for CLHEAR" (not "CLHEAR *X*").
- Reproduce the wordmark in plain text or the unaltered logo when linking to https://clhear.org.

## You may not

- Use the Marks in a way that suggests your product or organization is endorsed by, part of, or affiliated with CLHEAR or Reg42.
- Use the Marks, or confusingly similar marks, as or in a company name, product name, domain name or social handle ("CLHEAR Pro", "clhear-cloud.com").
- Alter the logo, combine it with other marks, or use it as the dominant element of your own branding.
- Use a conformance mark, or the words "CLHEAR conformant", "CLHEAR certified", "CL1" to "CL4" in relation to a program or product, unless the mark was granted through the conformance program and remains valid.

## Conformance marks

Conformance marks are granted **only** through the CLHEAR conformance program (`../conformance/`): CL1 and CL2 by verified self-assessment, CL3 and CL4 by an accredited independent assessor under ISAE 3000. A granted mark:

- names the assessed scope (organization, program, release of the standard) and its validity period;
- must be shown with that scope wherever it is used ("CLHEAR CL3 Assessed — AML program, UK entities, 2026");
- is withdrawn if the scope changes materially or the assessment lapses; the register of granted marks at https://clhear.org/conformance is authoritative.

## Descriptive use of the standard's outputs

Data outputs are licensed under ODC-By 1.0, which requires attribution. The attribution notice ("Contains CLHEAR data © Reg42 and contributors, licensed under ODC-By 1.0") is a permitted use of the name.

## Questions and permissions

Uses not covered here: trademark@clhear.org. Reg42 will not grant a use that a vendor-neutral standard could not grant to every vendor alike.

## Enforcement

Reg42 enforces the Marks to protect the standard's meaning: a mark that anyone can claim protects no one. Misuse is first addressed by a request to correct; the steering group is informed of any enforcement action.
