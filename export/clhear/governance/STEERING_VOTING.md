# How the steering group votes

Version 1.0 · licence CC BY 4.0

## Quorum

A vote is valid when at least two-thirds of the seats not abstaining for conflict are present (synchronously or by recorded asynchronous vote within the voting window), including at least one institution seat and at least one seat unaffiliated with Reg42.

## Thresholds

| Matter | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Roadmap priorities, forming or dissolving a working group, confirming reviewers | simple majority of votes cast |
| Adding or changing a conformance level, assessor accreditation criteria | two-thirds of votes cast |
| Charter amendment, licence change for any artefact class, trademark policy change | two-thirds of all seats, after a public RFC open for at least 30 days |
| Changes to the charter's principles (§2) or Reg42's commitments (§6) | two-thirds of all seats, and no more than one institution seat against |
| Removing a steering-group member for cause | two-thirds of all seats, the member concerned not voting |

Reg42's two permanent seats count as seats and vote; they do not carry a veto.

## Procedure

1. A matter is tabled as a GitHub Discussion in the *RFCs* category (or, for personnel matters, in the private steering channel) with a proposed resolution, the threshold that applies and the voting window (default 14 days; 30 days minimum for charter and licence matters).
2. Members declare conflicts (`CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md`) before voting; a declared conflict is an abstention.
3. Votes are recorded as *for*, *against*, *abstain* with an optional note. Asynchronous votes are posted in the Discussion; the chair tallies at the close of the window.
4. The result, the tally and the notes are published in the Discussion and in the next release notes under *Governance*.
5. A failed vote may be re-tabled after 90 days or when the proposal has materially changed.

## Chair

The chair (not from Reg42, elected annually by simple majority) tables matters, keeps the record, breaks ties on simple-majority matters, and represents the steering group publicly. The chair has no additional vote on qualified-majority matters.

## Emergency decisions

Where a public artefact contains restricted text or an organization identifier, the chair and one Reg42 seat may order its withdrawal immediately; the steering group ratifies within 7 days.
